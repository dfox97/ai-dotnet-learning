## Severity guide

- **Low:** cosmetic or mildly annoying; did not interrupt the task.
- **Medium:** caused hesitation, re-reading or a workaround, but the task remained clear.
- **High:** blocked progress, caused lost evidence, made the learner unsure what to do, or materially distorted the assessment/learning flow.
